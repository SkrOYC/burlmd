# Graph visualization

**Context:** CAP-GRAPH-06 proposed a graphical map of Note relationships.

**Decision:** Deferred.

**Reason:** Link traversal, ghost Links, and backlinks provide the required knowledge-graph workflows without a separate visualization surface.

**Consequences:** Remove CAP-GRAPH-06 from the active capability set. Reopening requires a Product Requirements Evolution pass.
