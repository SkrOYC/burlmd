# Actors

## Primary Actor: General Consumer (Knowledge Worker)
- **Role Name:** General Consumer
- **Operating Context:** Uses the application across multiple devices (desktop, mobile), frequently transitioning between connected and disconnected states. They are focused on content creation, research, and personal organization.
- **Concrete Goals:**
  - Frictionlessly capture and retrieve information.
  - Maintain a reliable, organized archive of their thoughts.
  - Keep notes synced effortlessly across their phone and computer.
  - Ensure their data isn't locked into a proprietary system they might lose access to.
- **User Frictions:**
  - Intimidated by technical tools, raw Markdown syntax, and Git concepts (commits, branches, diffs).
  - Frustrated by sync conflicts that require technical resolution or result in duplicated files.
  - Anxious about privacy and the longevity of cloud-only startups.
